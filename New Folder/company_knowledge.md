NomadGH is a U.S.-based trucking and logistics company. It provides freight transportation, dispatch coordination, and shipment support to business customers.

## Industry

Trucking, freight transportation, and logistics.

# Services

- Regional and long-haul freight transportation
- Driver dispatch and route coordination
- Shipment tracking and logistics support

# Locations

**Head office:** 1000 Demo Avenue, Chicago, IL 60601, USA

The company coordinates freight across the continental United States. Drivers may travel outside the Chicago area depending on their route.

# Careers

The following positions are currently open in this sample company profile.

## CDL-A Regional Truck Driver

**Location:** Chicago, Illinois, with regional travel

Transport freight on assigned regional routes, complete vehicle inspections, maintain required trip records, and communicate delivery updates to dispatch. Applicants need a valid CDL-A and must meet applicable commercial-driver qualification requirements. Previous commercial driving experience is preferred.

## Fleet Dispatcher

**Location:** Chicago, Illinois

Coordinate driver assignments and delivery schedules, monitor active loads, communicate route changes, and help resolve delays. Applicants should have clear communication and organizational skills. Dispatch or transportation experience is preferred.

## Logistics Coordinator

**Location:** Chicago, Illinois

Track shipments from pickup through delivery, maintain shipment documentation, coordinate with customers and carriers, and escalate service issues. Applicants should be comfortable with spreadsheets and routine customer communication. Logistics experience is preferred.

## Fleet Maintenance Technician

**Location:** Chicago, Illinois

Perform routine inspections, preventive maintenance, and repairs on fleet vehicles; document completed work and report safety concerns. Applicants should have practical vehicle-maintenance experience and be able to use common shop tools. Relevant training or certifications are preferred.

# Working conditions

Truck-driving work involves regional travel and schedules based on assigned routes. Dispatch, logistics, and maintenance roles are based at the Chicago office. Schedules and requirements are discussed during the hiring process. Remote work is not listed as a standard arrangement in this sample profile.
